The full questionnaire as it appears in data/survey.xlsx, grouped by section: 42 numbered questions in 26 blocks. Questions that share one instruction are shown once, with their items listed under it. "Answered (all / cleaned)" counts respondents who answered the question: all submissions that reached it, then the cleaned sample (tier B). Answer options come from the form definition (multiple choice) or from the distinct answers in the data (single choice and ratings).
