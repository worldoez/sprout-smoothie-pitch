Sprout · CUHK smoothie study

Sprout survey (copy for reference)

The full questionnaire as shown to CUHK students for the Sprout smoothie project — 37 questions in five parts. This page exists so anyone scanning the QR code on slide 8 of the pitch can see exactly what we asked.

Answers are not collected here. This is a read-only copy of the survey. Every input is disabled; nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere. The live study was a separate Google Form; results were analysed from that form only. Percentages shown next to options come from the cleaned dataset (n = 53) and are there for reference.
