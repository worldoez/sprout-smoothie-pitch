Our story, farm to cup

Every drink travels the same four steps, and you can walk past each one.

  1. Plant

    Seeds go into soil trays the week before the pop-up.

  2. Water

    A daily pour, and fruit bowls are refilled from the market.

  3. Harvest

    Fruit is cut the hour you order, never the night before.

  4. Blend

    Thirty seconds later the cup is yours, lid and straw on.
