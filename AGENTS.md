# Guide for agents and teammates editing this site

Read `PLAN.md` first (the vision, the 21-slide storyline, the order system).

## How to update slide text from a pasted doc (the reconcile workflow)
1. `SCRIPT.md` is the **source of truth for words**. Each slide has a block `## <n>. <title>` with: kicker, title, points, speaker notes, sources.
2. When the team pastes new text (from a Google Doc etc.), update `SCRIPT.md` first, keeping the block format and slide numbers.
3. Then reconcile code to it: each slide lives in `deck/slides/0X-<stage>.js` as a `Kit.content({...})` or `Deck.add({...})` block with the same `n:`. Change only the strings (title, kicker, points, notes, source). Do not touch animation code unless asked.
4. Numbers must come from `content/data.js` (generated by `python3 tools/sync_data.py` from `../output/tables`) or `../output/team_report_by_role.html`. Never invent a survey number.
5. Check: `for f in deck/slides/*.js; do node --check $f; done`, then open `deck/index.html` (serve with `python3 -m http.server`).

## Slide API (deck/engine.js)
`Deck.add({ n, stage, title, budget(sec), notes, html(ctx), steps:[(el,ctx)=>gsap.timeline()], ambient, usesOrder, onOrder(el,order,ctx), music, interactive, printFix })`
- `steps[0]` plays on entry; each click plays the next step; a click during a step fast-forwards it.
- Stage order: plant, water, harvest, blend, pour, serve, cheers. A stage change plays the liquid wipe.
- Slides render on a fixed 1920x1080 canvas. Use px sizes; the engine scales it.
- Sounds: `ctx.sfx("pop" | "whoosh" | "splat" | "drop" | "thunk" | "tada" | "riser" | "pour" | "water" | "blender" | "sparkle" | "seed" | "cheer" | "step" | "swoosh" | "click" | "sad")`.
- Print/PDF (`deck/index.html?print`) runs every step to its end, so the final frame of each slide must read well on its own.

## Design rules (from the anti-slop / design-engineering skills the team chose)
- One memorable motion idea per slide that *acts out the content* (seed grows, fruit races, liquid pours). No generic fade-up on everything.
- Animation beats ≤ 3 s. Use `power3.out`/`back.out` for entrances, `power2.in` for exits; never `linear` for UI movement except loops.
- Text appears after the action, in at most 3 short points (≤ 14 words each), with the number bolded.
- Big type: titles 96–150 px, points 34–40 px on the 1920 canvas. Minimum 20 px for sources.
- Colour per stage (see `STAGES` in engine.js). Accent sparingly. Contrast must stay readable on a projector.
- No ALL-CAPS labels, no "→" on every button, no middle-dot meta strings, no identical card grids.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` on the website. Touch targets ≥ 44 px on the order app.
