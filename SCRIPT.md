How to use — read this first
1. Paste new text here first (from the team Google Doc), keeping the block format and the slide numbers.
2. Then reconcile `deck/slides/0X-<stage>.js` to match: change strings only (kicker, title, points, notes, source). Never the animation code.
3. Numbers come from `content/data.js` (generated by `python3 tools/sync_data.py` from `../output/tables`) or `../output/team_report_by_role.html`. Never invent a survey number.
4. Check your work: `for f in deck/slides/*.js; do node --check "$f"; done`
5. Then open `deck/index.html` (serve with `python3 -m http.server`) and check every slide, plus `deck/index.html?print` for the PDF view.
